Your data stays yours.

These rules are built into the code and checked by automatic tests every time it changes. They aren't a policy we might forget.

  • Not kept
    Data from your database. Previews and query results are wiped 15 minutes after you see them. Excel files are deleted after 1 hour. Only table names and row counts stay, so the card keeps working.
  • 2 minutes
    Live view of your database (queries running right now, slowest queries) is shown and then deleted.
  • 30 days
    Activity log. What Goal Engine did and when, with values hidden (e.g. WHERE email = ?). No data from your tables.
  • Never changed
    Your database, unless you switch on approved changes. Every query is checked twice and runs in a read-only transaction. Commands that change data are refused.
  • Only after Apply
    Approved changes (off by default). You switch them on per database. Each change shows exactly which rows it will touch, runs only after you tap Apply, and saves the old rows first so Undo can put them back for 7 days. With the server connector or the website file, that copy stays on your own server; with typed details it's kept encrypted, then deleted.
  • Encrypted
    Database passwords and customer lists you paste. Stored encrypted, used only while a job runs. A database password you type is kept only while your database area is open: it's deleted when the area locks (15 minutes without use), when you log out, or when you disconnect.
  • On your server
    With the one-command connector, your database password never leaves your own server at all.
  • 24 hours
    Customer names, emails and message text inside an agent's results are removed after a day. Only the counts stay.
  • 30 days
    The "already emailed" log keeps a one-way fingerprint of each address (not the address) to avoid double messages, then it's deleted.
  • 90 days
    Your conversations and goals are deleted after 90 days without activity, unless an agent of yours is still switched on. With a free account they stay until you delete them.
  • Until you delete
    Your free account. Your name, phone, email, city, username and what you wrote about your work, so the agents can fit their answers to you. Your password is stored only as a one-way scrambled form (nobody, including us, can read it). Email codes work once, for 10 minutes. "Delete all my data" while logged in deletes the account too.
  • Only to reach you
    Expert requests. If you ask to talk to an expert, your name, number or email and what you need go to Chetan Sharma, only to contact you about it. Deleted after 180 days, or at once with "Delete all my data".
  • To learn
    Teaching the agents. When an agent isn't sure, it asks what you meant. If you pick an answer or tap "None of these", or if it couldn't answer your question directly, only that one sentence is kept, with emails, links, numbers and passwords removed, and without who sent it, so it can answer it straight away next time. Picks are deleted after 90 days, the other sentences after 180 days.
  • 180 days
    How you like to work. Your language, whether you want formulas, and whether the agent should ask before changing anything, so a returning visit starts right. Never what's in your files or messages. Deleted after 180 days without use, or at once with "Delete all my data". To get better at guessing the next question, the agents count only the kind of question (like "total by group, then the top one"), never your words or files. Those counts are anonymous, except your own habits, which are kept under a one-way code of your browser (not your name, email or the browser id itself) so a returning visit gets your usual next steps.
  • No AI service
    Your messages aren't sent to any outside AI. Goal Engine understands them with its own model, on its own server. Nothing is sold or shared.
