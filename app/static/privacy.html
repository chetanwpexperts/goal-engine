Your data stays yours.

These rules are built into the code and checked by automatic tests every time it changes. They aren't a policy we might forget.

  • Not kept
    Data from your database. Previews and query results are wiped 15 minutes after you see them. Excel files are deleted after 1 hour. Only table names and row counts stay, so the card keeps working.
  • Never changed
    Your database itself. Every query is checked twice and runs in a read-only transaction. Commands that change data are refused.
  • Encrypted
    Database passwords and customer lists you paste. Stored encrypted, used only while a job runs. A saved database password is deleted when you disconnect, or after 30 days without use.
  • On your server
    With the one-command connector, your database password never leaves your own server at all.
  • 24 hours
    Customer names, emails and message text inside an agent's results are removed after a day. Only the counts stay.
  • 30 days
    The "already emailed" log keeps a one-way fingerprint of each address (not the address) to avoid double messages, then it's deleted.
  • 90 days
    Your conversations and goals are deleted after 90 days without activity, unless an agent of yours is still switched on.
  • No AI service
    Your messages aren't sent to any outside AI. Goal Engine understands them with its own model, on its own server. Nothing is sold or shared.
