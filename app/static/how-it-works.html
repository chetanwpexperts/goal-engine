How it works

A model to understand.
Real engines to prove.

A trained classifier works out what you are asking. Written rules and real database engines decide what is true, so every answer comes with evidence instead of a guess.

  1. UnderstandYour message, in English, Hindi or Hinglish, is classified by Goal Engine's own model. Spelling slips are expected.
  2. ReadA query is split into blocks: the rows each step keeps, what it groups, where a join multiplies rows.
  3. ProveThe suspected cause is reproduced on real MariaDB and PostgreSQL engines, read-only, and the answer says how sure it is.
  4. FixYou see the change and the rows it touches first. Nothing runs without your approval, and every change has Undo.

The model

Small, fast and trained on confirmed examples.

  • Character and word n-grams with TF-IDF, then logistic regression. Character n-grams cope with Hinglish spellings like "chal rha" and "chl raha".
  • It retrains in about a second and runs on Goal Engine's own server. No outside AI service is called.
  • When three different people confirm what a sentence meant, it becomes a training example and the model retrains within minutes. One person alone can't teach it, so it can't be poisoned.
  • Only that one sentence is kept, with emails, numbers and passwords removed, and without who sent it.
